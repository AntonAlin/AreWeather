What this view does differently

Scoring ten peaks means ten sets of requests, so this page deliberately fetches less per mountain than the single-peak view: surface fields from all six models, plus snow depth and freezing level, but no pressure-level sounding. Summit temperature and wind are identical either way — the sounding is anchored to the surface ensemble at exactly summit height — but the cloud-base estimate is rougher here, so a summit in cloud penalty can differ by a few points from the detail page.
